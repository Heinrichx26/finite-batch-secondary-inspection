- user_identifier
Randomly assigned ID for a participant. 

- type_of_tv
The type of AI error that the participant encountered in the Trust Violation phase
False Alarm: There are no illicit objects in the baggage, but the AI indicated there is an illicit object.
False Miss: There is an illicit object in the baggage, but the AI indicated there are no illicit objects.

- type_of_responsive
The strategy of conveying XAI to participants.
R0: Non-responsive-all
R1: Responsive-all
R2: Non-responisve-difficult

- type_of_vis
The representation of XAI (saliency map or similar instances)

- s1_postSurvey_benefit_perception
The participant's self-reported score for 'Please indicate how beneficial you perceive it is to use the AI for completing the task well'.

- s1_postSurvey_risk_perception
The participant's self-reported score for 'Please indicate how risky you perceive it is to use the AI for completing the task well'.

- s1_postSurvey_explanation_help_judge
The participant's self-reported score for 'The explanation of the AI help me judge when I should trust and not trust the AI'.

- s1_postSurvey_explanation_help_understand
The participant's self-reported score for 'The explanation of the AI help me understand how the illicit objects detection is made'.

- s1_postSurvey_predict_behave
The participant's self-reported score for 'I can predict how the AI will behave'.

- s1_postSurvey_quick_familiar
The participant's self-reported score for 'I quickly became familiar with the AI'.

- s1_postSurvey_understand_task
The participant's self-reported score for 'I understand the AI for illicit objects detection'.
