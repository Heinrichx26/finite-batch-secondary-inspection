# Third-party data

The five original response-data files and their descriptions under `data/raw/human_backup/`
come from Yixuan Wang, Jieqiong Zhao, Yang Ba, Michelle V. Mancenido, Erin K. Chiou, Ross Maciejewski, *Impact of EXplainable AI on Trust Evolution with AI Error Severity: Comparing Similar Instances and Saliency Map in a Baggage Screening Task*, version 2,
Figshare, https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30316936.v2.
The data are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Original analysis inputs are redistributed unchanged; their MD5 checksums match
the publisher's Figshare metadata. Processed arrays and tables in `data/curated/`
were derived by the analysis code in this repository. Source data ownership and
attribution remain with the original dataset authors.

The source-paper references and audited document hashes in `data/literature/`
identify the methods used for comparison. Full-text publications are obtained
from their respective publishers.
